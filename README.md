# AI Growth Hub

High-Converting Capture Page (Landing Funnel)

Before users can access the app, create a professional, high-converting capture page designed to maximize email signups and conversions.

Hero Section

Display a modern full-screen hero with an animated background featuring AI, digital marketing, website traffic, social media, and business growth.

Headline:

Get More Website Traffic, Leads & Sales with AI—Starting Today!

Subheadline:

Discover the AI-powered marketing platform that helps entrepreneurs, affiliates, creators, coaches, and small business owners create high-converting content, attract more visitors, and grow their business in minutes.

Include an engaging promotional video prominently above the fold.

Video Player

Embed a large responsive video player with a modern design.

Display a professional thumbnail featuring:

AI marketing graphics

Website traffic analytics

Social media growth

Lead generation visuals

Call-to-action overlay

Include a large animated Play button.

Video Placeholder

Create a placeholder for a promotional video that can later be replaced with a YouTube, Vimeo, or self-hosted video.

Suggested video flow:

Welcome to TrafficBoost AI

The biggest problem business owners face is getting consistent traffic.

Show how AI creates marketing content in seconds.

Demonstrate social media post generation.

Demonstrate video script generation.

Demonstrate QR code generation.

Demonstrate lead magnet creation.

Show website analytics dashboard.

Display testimonials.

End with a strong invitation to join for free.

Duration:
60–90 seconds.
